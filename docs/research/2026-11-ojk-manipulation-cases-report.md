# OJK Stock-Manipulation Cases on the IDX, 2016 – November 2026: Label Dataset and Auto-Rejection History

I found 10 manipulation labels (stock and window) with dates stated by OJK or a court. Six were announced as sanctioned in 2026 (DSSA, PANR, FILM-2023, KIOS, SUPR, SHID). Three are older cases sanctioned in February 2026: IMPC and the BVN influencer case (AYLS, FILM, BSML). One was referred to prosecutors (SWAT). One more is under criminal investigation (BEBS). Only three or four of OJK's stated "29 cases, 2022 – February 2026" can be named from public sources. OJK has not published a per-case list for 2022–2025.

## TL;DR
- **Highest-confidence labels:** DSSA (three windows, 12 Jan 2024 – 3 Feb 2026), PANR (6 Sep 2022 – 29 May 2023), AYLS, FILM and BSML (BVN, 2021–2022), IMPC (Jan–Apr 2016), SHID (3 Jan – 14 Feb 2022), KIOS (Jan 2021 – Apr 2022), SUPR (8 Feb – 11 Apr 2022), FILM (24 Mar – 31 Jul 2023), SWAT (8 Jun – 5 Jul 2018) and BEBS (2020–2022, under investigation).
- **The 29-case figure reconciles with OJK's own numbers.** The January 2026 aggregate was Rp240.65bn from 151 parties. The 20 February 2026 sanctions added Rp11.05bn from 4 parties (BVN plus IMPC). That gives exactly Rp251.7bn and 155 parties. The other ~25–26 cases from 2022–2025 were reported only in aggregate, never by ticker.
- **Do not label these as manipulation:** BREN and CUAN (IPO allotment), REAL and PIPA (IPO funds, financial statements, allotment), and BOGA (misleading financial statements). The ARB/ARA regime changed five times between 2016 and April 2025, and a detector must account for the price-limit regime in force on each date.

## Details

**Reconciling OJK's "29 cases / 155 parties / Rp251.7bn".** On 9 Feb 2026, OJK reported Rp240.65bn in manipulation fines on 151 parties for 2022 to January 2026.\[47\]\[48\] The 20 Feb 2026 sanctions (SP 38) added Rp11.05bn on 4 parties.\[5\]\[15\] 240.65 + 11.05 = 251.70 and 151 + 4 = 155, so the stated totals are internally consistent. Of the 29 cases, the ones I could identify are SHID (letter dated 12 Jan 2026), IMPC and BVN (AYLS/FILM/BSML, which may count as one case or as three). That is 3 to 5 cases depending on OJK's counting convention. The remaining ~24–26 cases (2022–2025) appear only as aggregates. Example: OJK's December 2024 RDKB release, attributed to Inarno Djajadi and reported 7–8 Jan 2025 by Bloomberg Technoz, stated "total denda sebesar Rp14 miliar… Rp13,4 juta kepada 19 Pihak terkait pelanggaran Pasal 91 dan 92 UU PM… Rp600 juta kepada 12 Perusahaan Efek", without naming tickers; "Rp13,4 juta" is likely a typo for Rp13.4bn.

**Cases sanctioned February–November 2026 (after the 29-case cut-off).** These are DSSA, PANR, KIOS, FILM-2023 and SUPR. The 6 April 2026 RDK release said Rp15.9bn was fined to 6 individuals for market manipulation, plus 1 written warning to an individual.\[49\]\[50\] This matches KIOS (4 individuals, Rp5.9bn) plus FILM-2023 (2 individuals, Rp10bn), which equals 6 individuals and Rp15.9bn, plus the SUPR warning.\[9\]\[13\] That mapping is my inference from matching totals, not an OJK statement.

**Conflicts to flag in the dataset:**
- The BVN fine appears as Rp5.35bn in OJK's text.\[16\] Tempo's headline says "Rp5,3 M", a rounding.\[4\]\[51\]
- One SWA article gives Rp5.7bn as "total for all perpetrators" while the same piece says Rp11.05bn.\[52\] Rp5.7bn is the IMPC sub-total only.\[5\]
- DSSA's period: OJK's own SP 193 text says "perdagangan saham DSSA pada periode 2024 s.d. 2026" (versus "2023–2026" in Antara and Kontan coverage of the press conference) and states OJK summoned "setidaknya 12 Direksi Perusahaan Efek, 15 pihak perorangan, 11 badan hukum asing"; the sanction list gives three exact windows. Use the three windows.
- BVN's broker-name spelling also varies across reports.

**Investigations named by the user:**
- BEBS: criminal investigation.
- REAL and PIPA: administrative sanctions were for IPO and disclosure breaches, not trading manipulation.\[39\]\[53\] Any trading-manipulation probe of them has not been announced as concluded.
- IPPE: sanctioned on 28 Feb 2026, but I could not verify the nature of the violation.\[43\]
- In written answers for the July 2026 RDKB (30 Jul 2026, Antara), Hasan Fawzi, OJK Chief Executive for Capital Market Supervision, said: "Per 21 Juli 2026, sebanyak 5 kasus dalam proses pemeriksaan, 10 kasus dalam proses penyelesaian pemeriksaan, dan 17 kasus telah selesai dilakukan pemeriksaan"; the stocks were not identified.

## Recommendations
- Use rows 1, 2 and 6–10 as positive labels with exact dates. Treat KIOS and IMPC as month-granularity windows.
- Keep SWAT and BEBS in a separate "alleged/criminal" class until there is a verdict.
- Exclude the Jiwasraya and Asabri tickers from window-based evaluation, because no window is stated.
- When computing returns or abnormal-move features, condition on the auto-rejection regime in force on each date. ARB at 7% (2020–2023) truncates crash signatures.\[68\]
- To fill the 2022–2025 gap, file an e-PPID information request with OJK for the per-case list behind the Rp240.65bn figure.

## Caveats
- I could not open OJK's 31 Oct 2026 sanction list directly. The KIOS, SUPR, SHID, FILM-2023 and DSSA-window details come from multiple news outlets quoting it.\[9\]\[10\]\[69\]\[70\] Some (trenharapan, journalarta) are low-tier aggregators; the CNBC Indonesia, Katadata and Kontan versions are consistent with them.
- I list personal names only as initials or party types, per OJK practice and the user's instruction.
- Gaps: release numbers for SHID, KIOS, FILM-2023 and SUPR; per-ticker identities of the ~24–26 cases from 2022–2025; the IPPE violation type; the full Asabri stock list; and OJK annual-report enforcement chapters for 2022–2025, which I did not retrieve.
