# P0-03: Watchlist-board (full call auction) regime

- **Phase:** P0
- **Model:** sonnet
- **Deps:** P0-02
- **Size:** ~40 lines changed

## Goal
Add limits for stocks on the Papan Pemantauan Khusus (watchlist board), which have traded by full call auction since 2024-03-25. Without them, `price_limits()` gives wrong answers for those stocks.

## Files
- `data/price_limit_regimes.csv`
- `tests/test_api.py`
- `README.md` (regime section: document the `board` argument and remove the "main and development boards only" limitation bullet; replace it with a note that the Acceleration Board is not yet covered)

## Behaviour
Add regime `R2024-FCA` with `board=watchlist`, from 2024-03-25 with `effective_to` blank:
- (0,10]: fixed Rp1 / Rp1
- (10,): 10% / 10%

Decree `Peng-00001/BEI.PB1/03-2024`. Use the source URL from the research notes.

Notes: "Stage II full call auction. Stage I (2023-06-12) traded continuously and is not modelled. A July 2026 proposal for 35/25/20% limits was not confirmed adopted. Per news reports; decree not read."

## Acceptance
- `price_limits(date(2024,3,25), 8, board="watchlist")` gives fixed 1/1.
- `price_limits(date(2025,1,2), 300, board="watchlist")` gives 10/10.
- `price_limits(date(2024,3,22), 300, board="watchlist")` raises `ValueError`.
- The regular-board results are unchanged, and the whole suite passes.

## Out of scope
Acceleration Board rows: the research has no exact launch date, so a later verification chunk adds them. No code changes.
