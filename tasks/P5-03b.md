# P5-03b: Recovery difficulty sweep with a random-ranker baseline

- **Phase:** P5
- **Model:** sonnet
- **Deps:** P5-03, P5-01b
- **Size:** ~110 lines

## Goal
P5-03 gives every detector a recall of 1.0, because its default pump (+15% a day on 8× volume) is a cartoon. A sweep over weaker pumps, compared against a random ranker, shows where each detector stops finding pumps and whether it beats chance.

## Behaviour
- In `run_recovery`, each injected `Pump` uses `daily_return`, `volume_mult` and `dump_return = -0.8 * daily_return`. Pump and dump lengths stay at their defaults. With the defaults, the result for a seed must equal the current output exactly.
- `include_random=True` adds `"random"`: the recall of `random_ranks(composite ranks, seed=seed)` (P5-01b) scored the same way.
- `recovery_sweep` calls `run_recovery(..., include_random=True)` for every combination and averages recall over `seeds`. Detectors are rank_ret, rank_vol, rank_rule, composite and random. Rows are sorted by (daily_return, volume_mult, detector).

## Acceptance
- `test_defaults_unchanged`: `run_recovery(seed=0)` returns the same dict as before this chunk (all four values 1.0, no "random" key).
- `test_sweep_shape`: the default sweep returns 3 × 2 × 5 = 30 rows with the right columns.
- `test_strong_cell`: in the sweep, at (0.15, 8.0), composite recall ≥ 0.9.
- `test_beats_random_when_strong`: at (0.15, 8.0), each of the four detectors' recall exceeds random's by at least 0.3.
- `test_monotone_composite`: composite's mean recall over `volume_mults` at 0.15 is ≥ its mean at 0.03.
- `test_sweep_runtime`: the default sweep finishes in under 90 seconds.

If a threshold fails, report the full sweep table. Do **not** loosen thresholds or tweak detectors. The planner decides.
