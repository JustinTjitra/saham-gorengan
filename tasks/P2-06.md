# P2-06: Range volatility with limit-day censoring

- **Phase:** P2
- **Model:** sonnet
- **Deps:** P2-01
- **Size:** ~110 lines

## Goal
A volatility feature (PLAN.md 3.2, feature 6) based on the daily high-low range (Parkinson). Days that close at ARA or ARB have a range cut off by the limit, so they are left out of the estimate and counted separately.

## Files
- `src/saham_gorengan/features/volatility.py` (new)
- `tests/test_features_volatility.py` (new)

## Interface
```python
def volatility_features(bars: pd.DataFrame, window: int = 20, min_periods: int = 10,
                        board: str = "regular") -> pd.DataFrame: ...
    """Columns: date, ticker, parkinson (float64), vol_20 (float64), censored_share_20 (float64).
    Same rows and order as validate_bars(bars)."""
```

## Behaviour
- Call `validate_bars` first and never modify the input. Windows count each ticker's rows.
- `parkinson = ln(high / low)^2 / (4 ln 2)` for every row.
- A row is **censored** when `limit_features(bars, board)` (P2-01) gives `ara_hit` or `arb_hit` True. Rows where both are NA, such as a ticker's first row, are not censored.
- `vol_20 = sqrt(mean(parkinson))` over the uncensored rows among the ticker's last `window` rows including D. NaN when fewer than `min_periods` uncensored rows are available.
- `censored_share_20`: the share of censored rows among the last `window` rows including D. NaN until the ticker has `window` rows.
- `window` < 2, or `min_periods` outside 1..`window`, raises `ValueError`. Column names keep the `_20` suffix; document this.
- No look-ahead.

## Acceptance
`tests/test_features_volatility.py`:
- `test_constant_range`: one ticker for 20 rows in 2024 with close 100, `high = 100 * e^0.05` and `low = 100 * e^-0.05` (so `ln(high/low) = 0.1`) and no limit hits. Row 20 has `parkinson ≈ 0.0036067` and `vol_20 ≈ 0.0600561`.
- `test_limit_days_excluded`: the same ticker where rows 11-20 close at ARA (each close is 1.35 × the previous close, under the 2024 35% tier while the price is ≤ 200; use a starting price of 10 so it stays ≤ 200), with a wide range on those days. `censored_share_20` on row 20 is 0.5, and `vol_20` on row 20 equals the value computed from rows 1-10 only.
- `test_min_periods`: with `min_periods=10`, `vol_20` is NaN on rows 1-9 and set from row 10.
- `test_bad_args`: `window=1`, `min_periods=0` and `min_periods=window + 1` raise.
- `test_no_lookahead`: `assert_no_lookahead(volatility_features, synthetic_bars(n_tickers=5))` passes.
- `test_input_not_modified`.

## Out of scope
Close-to-close volatility; intraday data.
