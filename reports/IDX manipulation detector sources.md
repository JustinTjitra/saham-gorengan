# Build the gorengan detector on IDX's own tape

**Research date:** 20 November 2026. **Sources:** the four notes in `research_notes/IDX manipulation detector sources/` plus the earlier label report `docs/research/2026-11-ojk-manipulation-cases-report.md`. **Reading caveat:** idx.co.id, ojk.go.id, Yahoo, Stooq, KSEI and several news hosts were blocked from the research sessions. Most findings therefore rest on search-engine extracts of the cited pages and on the source code of public GitHub scrapers. No IDX decree PDF was read in full, and no data endpoint was tested live.

The best free base for saham-gorengan is IDX's own undocumented `GetStockSummary` endpoint. One request returns the whole market for one day, including the fields a manipulation detector needs and Yahoo lacks: trade frequency, traded value, foreign buy and sell, non-regular (negotiated) volume, listed and tradeable shares, and bid/offer queues. It comes with three constraints. IDX's terms of use forbid scraping. A Cloudflare layer blocks datacenter IPs. And nobody has shown that its archive reaches back to 2015, so the first engineering task is a live probe of 5 January 2015 from a residential or self-hosted machine. Yahoo Finance is a usable gap-filler and cross-check, but it drops delisted stocks, which are exactly the stocks a gorengan detector most needs. The 13 sanctioned OJK windows can only evaluate a detector, not train one. Exchange signals add thousands of weaker labels, which can be ranked in order of severity from ARA streaks up to court verdicts, but most of them are triggered by price extremes, so they cannot serve as an independent test. The method that fits is a transparent robust-z-score screen plus an unsupervised anomaly model, scored by event-level recall against placebo windows and by lead time ahead of IDX's own flags. The most urgent fix is `data/price_limit_regimes.csv`. It has been wrong since 28 September 2026: three new limit regimes in four weeks introduced a Rp1 minimum price, fixed-rupiah limits and a 7% ARB, and the current schema (percentages only, one board) cannot represent them.

## One undocumented IDX endpoint carries almost every field a detector needs

**`GetStockSummary?date=YYYYMMDD` is the strongest free candidate for the daily panel.** Scrapers active in September to November 2026 show it returning the full cross-section for one date in a single call ([Ridzz05/idx-whale-tracker](https://github.com/Ridzz05/idx-whale-tracker); [nichsedge/idx-bei issue #26](https://github.com/nichsedge/idx-bei/issues/26)). The IDX field schema, read from scraper source code, includes `Previous, OpenPrice, High, Low, Close, Volume, Value, Frequency, Bid, BidVolume, Offer, OfferVolume, ListedShares, TradebleShares, ForeignBuy, ForeignSell, NonRegularVolume, NonRegularValue, NonRegularFrequency` and `DelistingDate` ([antonizer/IDX-Scrapper](https://github.com/antonizer/IDX-Scrapper/blob/master/get-data-harian-emiten.py)). Volume, share counts and foreign flows are in shares, not lots ([SeaMCP tool description](https://glama.ai/mcp/connectors/io.github.SeaMCP/idx/tools/Idx_get_company)). `TradebleShares` is not regulatory free float and should not be used as one ([idx-flow-scanner PR #52](https://github.com/rizanrizan93/idx-flow-scanner/pull/52)). The same project cross-checked 400 tickers on 2 September 2026 against a paid vendor and found **zero difference in foreign buy, foreign sell, volume and value**. That is the only independent accuracy check found for the endpoint ([idx-flow-scanner PR #52](https://github.com/rizanrizan93/idx-flow-scanner/pull/52)).

Each daily record carries a `DelistingDate`, so if the archive reaches 2015 it would remove Yahoo's survivorship bias. That depth is still unknown. No project documents a backfill to 2015, and the earliest date the endpoint returns is not stated anywhere the research could reach. Sister endpoints on the same host cover company profiles (`GetCompanyProfiles`), corporate actions in 15 categories (`GetIssuedHistory`), per-broker aggregates (`GetBrokerSummary`), and disclosures (`GetAllAnnouncement`) ([nichsedge/idx-bei README](https://github.com/nichsedge/idx-bei)). `GetIssuedHistory` is the free source for the split and rights-issue factors needed to adjust prices.

**Access is the hard part.** Plain HTTP, curl and a separately launched headless Chrome all get 403. One working approach runs `fetch()` inside a real Chrome tab that has passed the Cloudflare challenge. In that project, six parallel requests produced 29 HTTP 429s and 6 HTTP 403s out of 50, and the safe pace was one request every 1.5 seconds, with a 10-minute pause after any 429 or 403 ([idx-whale-tracker](https://github.com/Ridzz05/idx-whale-tracker)). Another project reports that idx.co.id refuses requests from Cloudflare Workers and from cloud networks, "GitHub's own runners included", so it runs that job on a self-hosted runner ([fzn0x/idx](https://github.com/fzn0x/idx)). The `idx-bei` project passes the WAF by impersonating browser TLS fingerprints (`curl_cffi`, Go uTLS) and rotating profiles, treating 403 as retryable, and logging `None` as an error while only `[]` means a non-trading day. That pipeline was silently stale from 15 to 18 September 2026 because 403s had been logged as "no data (non-trading day?)" ([idx-bei issue #26](https://github.com/nichsedge/idx-bei/issues/26)). The fail-closed guards in idx-flow-scanner are a good template for daily ingest validation: reject payloads with fewer than 900 stocks, reject date mismatches, and skip weekends and holidays ([PR #52](https://github.com/rizanrizan93/idx-flow-scanner/pull/52)). A full backfill takes fewer than 3,000 daily requests, a little over an hour at the safe pace, so access, not volume, is the constraint.

**The terms are restrictive.** IDX's Syarat Penggunaan allows non-commercial citation with the source and access date, but states that web scraping and crawling are not permitted, and commercial use or redistribution needs written permission ([IDX Syarat Penggunaan](https://www.idx.co.id/id/syarat-penggunaan), via search extract; quoted the same way in [wildangunawan/Dataset-Saham-IDX](https://github.com/wildangunawan/Dataset-Saham-IDX)). For a non-commercial research project, the defensible position is low request volume, attribution, and no redistribution of raw data. That matches the repo's existing rule that market data is never committed. Commercial use means buying the official product: IDX publishes data-service price lists dated 2021 and September 2024 ([IDX price list](https://www.idx.co.id/media/9649/idx-data-services-price-list-version-02-add-eod.pdf); [IDX catalogue 2024](https://www.idx.co.id/media/2qalvu4z/20240923_idx-data-catalogue-pricelist-2024.pdf)), and ICE resells IDX history including up to 15 years of tick data ([ICE](https://developer.ice.com/fixed-income-data-services/catalog/indonesia-stock-exchange-idx)).

**Yahoo Finance (`.JK`) is the fallback, not the base.** It has no official API. Tickers that were delisted, acquired or went bankrupt "mostly return nothing", which biases any universe built from it towards survivors ([marketparquet](https://marketparquet.com/guides/yfinance-limitations-backtesting)). yfinance's `history()` defaults to `auto_adjust=True` and `repair=False` ([yfinance reference](https://ranaroussi.github.io/yfinance/reference/yfinance.price_history.html)). The detector should therefore request unadjusted OHLC and apply its own adjustment factors: silently adjusted prices break the arithmetic for detecting ARA and ARB hits. In 2025 Yahoo began rate-limiting heavily, blocked IPs for up to a day after bursts, and failed the cookie/crumb handshake ([yfinance discussion #2431](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/discussions/2431)). It also has platform-wide gaps, such as missing history for 5 November 2025 ([yfinance issue #2621](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/issues/2621)).

**KSEI is the easiest official host to fetch, and from March 2026 it is the ownership source.** Since 3 March 2026, KSEI and IDX publish every shareholder above 1% for all listed stocks each month, under OJK Board Decision 1/KDK.04/2026. Before that, only holdings above 5% were public ([KSEI press release](https://web.ksei.co.id/files/uploads/press_releases/press_file/id-id/252_bei_dan_ksei_terbitkan_informasi_kepemilikan_saham_perusahaan_tercatat_di_atas_1_20260303225811.pdf); [Kontan](https://investasi.kontan.co.id/news/klik-idxcoididberita-publik-bisa-lihat-investor-kakap-pemegang-saham-di-atas-1)). One working tool reports that ksei.co.id "has no bot protection" ([fzn0x/idx](https://github.com/fzn0x/idx)). For the 2015 to 2025 backtest there is no free history of ownership concentration or free float, so the detector has to fall back on `TradebleShares/ListedShares` as an explicitly non-regulatory proxy. The concentration features from the 1% files can only be added for live scoring.

Other free sources do not add much. The best pre-built bulk set is a Kaggle EOD dataset whose ticker count and delisted coverage are unverified ([Baselight mirror](https://baselight.app/u/kaggle/dataset/pholenk_eod_data_indonesia_stock_exchange)). A CC BY-NC dataset on GitHub reportedly covers 958 stocks from 2019 to 2025 ([saham-mcp README](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/@baguskto/saham-mcp/blob/b9956333309d08f677afc4f8a23644fbdc223cdf/README.md)). Stooq's IDX coverage could not be confirmed. Broker-level flow, which is what exposes a single broker cycling volume, is only partly free. IPOT exposes a top-10 broker summary per stock but returns bursts of spurious 404s, and Stockbit's full broker summary needs the user's own logged-in session ([fzn0x/idx](https://github.com/fzn0x/idx)), which is unsuitable for an unattended pipeline.

| Source | Key fields | History | Headless access from cloud | Terms | Role |

| IDX `GetStockSummary` | OHLC, volume, value, frequency, foreign flow, non-regular, bid/offer, shares, delisting date | Unknown; test 2015-01-05 | Hard: TLS impersonation or real browser, 1 req/1.5 s, cloud IPs blocked | Scraping prohibited; non-commercial citation only | Primary panel |

The project's cloud environment must allowlist at least `www.idx.co.id`, `idx.co.id`, `query1.finance.yahoo.com`, `query2.finance.yahoo.com`, `fc.yahoo.com`, `guce.yahoo.com`, `www.ksei.co.id` and `web.ksei.co.id` before anything can be fetched. Even after that, IDX's own blocking of datacenter IPs means the IDX fetcher will probably need to run on a residential or self-hosted machine and sync Parquet files to the cloud.

## Exchange surveillance yields thousands of weak labels, but they echo price extremes

The 13 sanctioned windows are too few to learn from. IDX's public surveillance trail is much larger, and it orders naturally by severity. **Unusual Market Activity (UMA) notices** are the broadest layer. IDX issued about 50 in 2015 and 128 in 2016 ([Kontan](https://investasi.kontan.co.id/news/uma-melonjak-lebih-dari-100-di-2016)), 92, 127 and 177 in 2021 to 2023 ([Owner journal](https://owner.polgan.ac.id/index.php/owner/article/view/2567)), then **262 in 2024 and 528 by 24 December 2025**, including 85 in September 2025 alone ([EmitenNews](https://emitennews.com/news/surat-uma-bei-melonjak-drastis-saham-liar-makin-banyak)). IDX publishes no numeric triggers. An official described weighing price, orders, volume, transaction patterns and material information ([Kontan](https://investasi.kontan.co.id/news/saham-cdia-dan-coin-masuk-uma-dan-kena-suspensi-begini-penjelasan-bei)), and IDX says a UMA does not imply a violation ([Mandiri Sekuritas](https://www.mandirisekuritas.co.id/en/siaran-pers-artikel/artikel/pengertian-uma-saham)). The founding academic study that used UMA as a manipulation proxy found no fundamental or trading differences from matched non-UMA stocks and no post-announcement reversal. It suggested that many UMAs reflect informed trading rather than manipulation ([Hanafi 2010, GamaIJB](https://journal.ugm.ac.id/gamaijb/article/view/5511)). The count rose by about half in 2024 and doubled in 2025, at the same time as the FCA watchlist and a tighter suspension policy, so UMA's base rate follows policy as well as behaviour. Normalise UMA counts by year before comparing periods.

**Price-driven suspensions are a stronger signal and can be classified by their wording.** IDX escalates to a suspension after a UMA fails to cool a stock. The notices use fixed phrases: "peningkatan/penurunan harga kumulatif yang signifikan" and "cooling down sebagai bentuk perlindungan bagi investor". Non-price suspensions cite late financial statements, unpaid listing fees, free-float breaches of Rule I-A, and similar grounds. An open scraper already parses these from a verified JSON endpoint, `primary/NewsAnnouncement/GetSuspension` ([supertypeai/sectors_idx_suspension](https://github.com/supertypeai/sectors_idx_suspension)). In practice the first suspension lasts about a day, the second about a week and often reopens on full call auction, and the third about a month. Media describe this pattern; no written IDX rule sets it ([Kontan Insight](https://insight.kontan.co.id/news/sama-seperti-daaz-ratu-disuspensi-sehari-oleh-bei-pasca-cetak-ara-berturut-turut); [EmitenNews](https://www.emitennews.com/news/dua-dari-tiga-saham-dirantai-bursa-usai-ara-bakal-dijerat-fca-sepekan)). Only price-reason suspensions should count as weak positives. Late-filer suspensions mostly hit illiquid penny stocks and are better used as controls for contamination.

**The label landscape shifted in November 2026.** Tempo reported that IDX would stop relying on suspensions and instead publish investigation announcements and freeze securities accounts ([Tempo](https://en.tempo.co/read/2122364/idx-to-stop-stock-suspensions-target-misbehaving-accounts)). The first three, around 6 November 2026, named **NSSS (review window 1 December 2025 to 30 October 2026), FUJI (2 March to 30 October 2026) and CASA (26 October to 2 November 2026)**. In CASA's case, six investors trading through six brokers made up about 98% of activity. IDX targets results within 45 days ([CNBC Indonesia](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/market/20261106215925-17-783128/transaksi-dikuasi-segelintir-pihak-bei-selidiki-3-saham-ini); [Bisnis](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20261110/7/2020197/bei-targetkan-investigasi-saham-casa-fuji-dan-nsss-rampung-45-hari); [Tempo](https://en.tempo.co/read/2123220/why-idx-probes-transactions-in-casa-fuji-and-nsss-shares)). These are exchange allegations, not findings. The repo's rules require them to be stored as a separate class and never described as manipulation.

**ARA streaks are the one weak label reproducible for every year from prices alone.** Cooling-down suspensions in 2025 followed runs of six consecutive ARA days for CDIA and COIN ([Investortrust](https://investortrust.id/market/72806/ara-6-hari-beruntun-bei-suspensi-saham-cdia-dan-coin)) and eight for KOCI ([EmitenNews](https://emitennews.com/news/sempat-2x-ara-8x-ara-beruntun-2-saham-disuspensi-bei-kapan-dibuka)). A label `ara_run_k` (k = 3 to 6 consecutive closes at the upper limit) can be computed for 2015 to 2026 once the regime table is correct. Recent IPOs dominate these runs, so days since listing must be a control. The special monitoring board (Papan Pemantauan Khusus, notation X) adds a further signal. Before 28 September 2026, its 11 criteria included trading-based ones, and the amendment that took effect that day removed criteria 1, 6, 7 and 10, moving 92 securities off the board and leaving 42 ([IDX Channel](https://www.idxchannel.com/market-news/bei-rombak-aturan-papan-pemantauan-khusus-4-kriteria-ini-dihapus); [Infobank](https://infobanknews.com/bei-ubah-aturan-papan-pemantauan-khusus-92-efek-keluar)). Fundamental notations (E for negative equity, L for late filings, and P for free-float shortfalls since SE-00009/BEI/07-2026 took effect on 3 August 2026) are better used as covariates or exclusion filters than as labels ([CNBC Indonesia](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/market/20260803153844-17-756144/bei-perbarui-notasi-khusus-saham-kini-ada-penanda-free-float-kurang)).

| Tier | Signal | Coverage | How to obtain | Main caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | UMA notice | 2015 onward, ~50 to 528 a year | `GetAnnouncement` (last 3 years only); news roundups and archives for earlier years | Triggered by price and volume extremes; many are informed moves |
| 1 | `ara_run_k` | 2015 onward, all stocks | Computed from prices plus regime table | Same circularity; dominated by IPOs |
| 2 | Price-reason suspension | 2015 to Oct 2026 | `GetSuspension` plus classifying the wording | Ex-post; ends as a policy tool in Nov 2026 |
| 3 | Repeat suspension, move to FCA | 2024 onward | Announcements | Practice, not written rule |
| 4 | IDX investigation announcement | Nov 2026 onward | News, IDX announcements | Allegation only; 3 cases so far |
| 5 | OJK sanction, court verdict | 2016 onward, 13 to 14 windows | `data/windows.csv` | Sparse; biased toward large, visible cases |

**History is the practical bottleneck.** The IDX announcement endpoint "serves a window of exactly three years, ending today", and IDX refers users to the paid TICMI archive for anything older. Keyword queries silently truncate results unless the client first reads `ResultCount` and then requests that page size. The client that documents this runs from a real Chromium profile at one request every 6.5 seconds ([gimanainidit/idx-reach](https://github.com/gimanainidit/idx-reach)). `GetSuspension` probably has the same limit, though that is untested. For 2015 to 2023, UMA and suspension history has to be rebuilt from daily news roundups ([paretosaham](https://www.paretosaham.com/2025/10/update-perdagangan-bursa-efek-indonesia-bei-daftar-saham-uma-unsuspend-dan-suspend-8-oktober-2025.html); [IPOT News](https://www.indopremier.com/ipotnews/newsDetail.php?jdl=BEI_Cabut_Suspensi_Tiga_Saham__Awasi_Pergerakan_Lima_Saham_Lain&news_id=205865&group_news=IPOTNEWS&news_date=&taging_subtype=STOCK&name=&search=y_general&q=suspensi,UMA&halaman=1)), from Wayback captures, and from thesis appendices. No machine-readable list for 2015 to 2026 exists. The weak-label tiers are uneven in time as a result: dense from late 2023 onward, sparse and hand-assembled before that.

**OJK's own totals show how incomplete the confirmed labels are.** OJK fined 151 parties Rp240.65bn for share-price manipulation from 2022 to January 2026 ([Republika](https://ekonomi.republika.co.id/berita/ta70ce370/ojk-denda-151-pihak-rp-24065-miliar-atas-manipulasi-saham); [Bisnis](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20260209/7/1951448/bersih-bersih-saham-gorengan-ojk-jatuhkan-sanksi-denda-ke-151-pihak)). It examined 155 cases in 2025, 116 of them involving stock trading ([Bisnis](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20251231/7/1940570/marak-kasus-pelanggaran-pasar-modal-2025-ojk-tegas-jatuhkan-sanksi)), and had **122 cases in process on 1 October 2026** ([Warta Ekonomi](https://wartaekonomi.co.id/amp/read643092/ojk-ancam-sanksi-berat-bagi-manipulator-saham-bisa-berujung-pidana)). Against roughly ten tickers that can be named, most of OJK's work never surfaces by ticker. Two leads could recover more: a 2019 OJK announcement sanctioning four individuals over a "kasus perdagangan saham" whose ticker was not captured ([OJK](https://ojk.go.id/id/berita-dan-kegiatan/pengumuman/Pages/OJK-Berikan-Sanksi-Terhadap-Vivilia-Valentina-Rizal-Andrika-Liauw-Mei-Tjin-dan-Abi-Said-atas-Kasus-Perdagangan-Saham.aspx)), and OJK annual-report enforcement chapters, which could not be opened.

## Thirteen windows support event-level evaluation, not supervised training

**International evidence points to the same daily signature.** Manipulated stocks are small, illiquid and often poorly governed. During the scheme they show abnormal returns, volume and volatility, and prices reverse afterwards. This holds for SEC cases ([Aggarwal & Wu 2006](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:ucp:jnlbus:v:79:y:2006:i:4:p:1915-1954)), for Taiwanese pump-and-dumps, which show temporary spikes, heavy volume and long-term reversal ([Huang & Cheng 2015](https://ideas.repec.org/a/kap/rqfnac/v44y2015i4p791-815.html)), and for an IDX study that found volume positively correlated with the cumulative abnormal return over the next four days ([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/344164378_Pump-Dump_Manipulation_Analysis_The_Influence_of_Market_Capitalization_and_Its_Impact_on_Stock_Price_Volatility_at_Indonesia_Stock_Exchange)). Indonesian work also documents pump-and-dump in early IPO trading ([event study](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/40be21b5983e57bea73a8562acfcab246755a37e)) and broker-driven run-ups ([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/305282282_Price_Manipulation_in_Indonesian_Capital_Market_Empirical_Analysis_on_Stockbroker's_Behavior_and_Interaction_Pattern_between_Domestic_Investors_and_Foreign_Investors)). No published IDX detector exists, so saham-gorengan's results should be framed as exploratory.

The base feature set follows from that signature. All features are computed per stock-day from data up to the close of day D:

- market-adjusted abnormal return and cumulative run-ups over 5 to 60 days;
- robust (median/MAD) z-scores of log volume, value and frequency against the stock's own trailing 60 to 120 days, with the MAD floored so that a jump from near-zero volume does not explode;
- range-based volatility from OHLC;
- price-volume coupling;
- post-peak drawdown;
- closing-price proxies: (C−L)/(H−L), close against VWAP (value divided by volume), and the next-day open reversal;
- average trade size (value divided by frequency), where many very small trades suggest fragmented or wash activity;
- non-regular volume share and net foreign flow;
- static vulnerability priors: price band, Amihud illiquidity, share of zero-volume days, listing age, board and notation.

The closing-price proxies come from the closing-price literature: about 1% of closes are manipulated, and only a small fraction of those cases are ever prosecuted ([Comerton-Forde & Putniņš 2014](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:oup:revfin:v:18:y:2014:i:1:p:23-66.); [Comerton-Forde & Putniņš 2011](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinin/v20y2011i2p135-158.html)). Their translation to end-of-day data is untested. Because raw anomaly scores concentrate in thin stocks, scores should be ranked within liquidity and size buckets per day.

**On methods, simple models trained on normal data hold up best when labels are few.** On SEC cases, Naive Bayes beat SVM, CART, KNN and random forest, reaching F2 of 53% and sensitivity of 89% ([Golmohammadi et al. 2014](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/7058109/)). The Thai SET series is the closest analogue to a regime with about 13 cases. An unsupervised autoencoder/GAN trained only on normal behaviour caught 5 of 6 prosecuted cases with a low false-positive rate ([Leangarun et al. 2021](https://nstda.or.th/openarchive/wp-content/uploads/tainacan-items/2494/99565/Stock_Price_Manipulation_Detection_Using_Deep_Unsupervised_Learning_The_Case_of_Thailand.pdf)). A transformer trained on synthesised pump-and-dump patterns also caught 5 of 6 ([KMUTT](https://kirim.kmutt.ac.th/converis/portal/detail/Publication/986842852?lang=en_GB)), and an ensemble caught all 6 ([KMUTT ESWA 2023](https://kirim.kmutt.ac.th/converis/portal/detail/Publication/1366062027?lang=en_GB)). Those figures come from abstracts with different setups and are not comparable with each other. They also used order-book data, not end-of-day data. PU learning, which treats labelled windows as positives and everything else as unlabelled ([Elkan & Noto 2008](https://doi.org/10.1145/1401890.1401920)), has been applied to Chinese accounting fraud ([XJTU](https://scholar.xjtu.edu.cn/en/publications/detecting-accounting-fraud-in-china-a-share-market-with-pu-learni/)) but never to trade-based manipulation. Its usual "selected completely at random" assumption is plainly violated here, because regulators prosecute large, visible cases. The design that fits saham-gorengan has four layers:

1. A MAD-z-score and ARA-streak screen as the baseline.
2. An isolation forest ([Liu et al. 2008](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICDM.2008.17)) per liquidity bucket.
3. Optionally, a heavily regularised PU ranker whose output is read as a rank, not a probability.
4. Change-point segmentation (PELT, [Killick et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.2012.737745)) of abnormal turnover, so that whole episodes are scored rather than single days.

Sequence models need far more positives and should wait.

**Evaluation is where a naive protocol fails, and the window lengths in `data/windows.csv` make the problem concrete.** The Thai studies report cases caught out of cases prosecuted, together with a false-alarm rate ([NSTDA](https://nstda.or.th/openarchive/wp-content/uploads/tainacan-items/2494/99565/Stock_Price_Manipulation_Detection_Using_Deep_Unsupervised_Learning_The_Case_of_Thailand.pdf)), and the obvious IDX version counts a window as caught if the stock enters the daily top-k on any day. That rule is far too lenient. A random top-20 list drawn from about 900 stocks picks a given stock with probability about 2.2% per day. Over a window of 20 trading days, the chance of at least one random hit is about 36%. Over PANR's window of roughly 175 trading days it is about 98%, and over DSSA-2024-3's nineteen months it is effectively 100%. Event-level recall therefore means nothing without a null. Each window should be scored against placebo windows of the same length, placed on random tickers in the same liquidity bucket and year. Alongside "any hit", the metrics should be the latency from window start to first alert and the share of window days flagged relative to that stock's out-of-window rate.

Month-precision windows (KIOS, IMPC) should be scored with multiple-instance logic, with a one-month buffer and no day-level precision. Any supervised layer should be validated leave-one-event-out, with roughly 60 trading days purged and embargoed around each held-out window. Three numbers should accompany every result: lead time ahead of the first UMA or suspension, which is the detector's real value over IDX surveillance; the daily alert budget; and "weak precision" against the union of tier 1 to 4 labels, reported separately from recall on OJK windows. Measured precision is a lower bound, because unlabelled stock-days contain many unprosecuted positives. One thesis estimate puts undetected or unprosecuted closing-price manipulation at about 300 cases for every prosecuted one ([Putniņš thesis](https://core.ac.uk/download/41234083.pdf)). A manual review of a random sample of top-k alerts is the most honest precision estimate available.

Two controls guard against circularity. First, every model must beat plain rankings by absolute abnormal return and by abnormal volume, plus a "UMA clone" rule. Second, days after a UMA or suspension should be excluded from training negatives and from evaluation, because surveillance flags themselves change later returns and volume ([Chari & Inamdar 2024](https://ideas.repec.org/a/wsi/rpbfmp/v27y2024i01ns0219091524500048.html)).

The labels also differ in kind, and evaluation should be split by scheme accordingly. They include influencer pumps (BVN across AYLS, FILM and BSML), nominee-account wash trading (IMPC, PANR, SWAT), and a structured multi-broker pump lasting about two years (DSSA). A detector tuned to short ARA bursts will miss slow structured accumulation.

**Price limits need explicit handling in features.** Returns should be normalised by the limit in force, with r divided by the ARA limit when r > 0 and by the ARB limit otherwise. Limit hits should be computed with tick rounding from the previous close. Streak counts and ARB-after-ARA-streak should be features. Volatility baselines should exclude limit days or use a censored estimator, so that a 7% ARB regime does not make downside volatility look artificially low ([Bisnis](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20261019/7/2013651/bei-pangkas-batas-auto-rejection-bawah-arb-jadi-asimetris-7)). Under asymmetric limits a dump has to play out over several ARB days, so multi-day post-peak drawdown is the more robust "dump" feature. Other pitfalls also need explicit treatment:

- survivorship, handled by building the universe point-in-time and including delisted tickers;
- deeply discounted rights issues, which look like crashes when unadjusted;
- zero-volume days, where returns should be computed from the last traded price and the days since that trade recorded;
- IPO pops, handled by excluding the first 20 to 60 sessions from baselines;
- index rebalancing dates;
- market-wide shock days, which are listed in the next section.

## Three price-limit regimes in four weeks break the repo's table

**The R2025-ARB15 regime (ARB 15% on every tier, Kep-00003/BEI/04-2025) ended on 25 September 2026, not open-ended as the CSV says.** Under Kep-00136/BEI/09-2026, from Monday 28 September 2026 the minimum price on the regular and cash markets fell from Rp50 to Rp1. A new Rp1 to Rp10 tier got a fixed Rp1 limit in both directions, and ARB stayed at 15% above Rp10 ([Media Indonesia](https://mediaindonesia.com/ekonomi/936319/bei-ubah-batas-auto-rejection-dan-harga-minimum-saham-rp1-per-28-september-2026); [Kontan](https://investasi.kontan.co.id/news/mengenal-kebijakan-baru-bei-soal-harga-minimum-saham-rp1-beserta-batas-ara-arb)). After IHSG fell about 8% at the open on 16 October and trading halted over MSCI's consultation on reclassifying Indonesia, Kep-00157/BEI/10-2026 cut ARB to **7% above Rp10 from 19 October 2026 to 31 December 2026**, leaving ARA and the halt rules unchanged ([CNBC Indonesia](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/market/20261019062711-17-777291/bei-terapkan-batas-auto-rejection-bawah-jadi-7-mulai-hari-ini); [Periskop](https://periskop.id/pasar-modal/20261019/arb-7-persen-trading-halt-jeffrey-hendrik)). A day later, Kep-00158/BEI/10-2026 set a fixed Rp1 ARB for Rp11 to Rp14 stocks ([Media Indonesia](https://mediaindonesia.com/ekonomi/946380/bei-sesuaikan-batas-arb-saham-berharga-rp11rp14-menjadi-rp1); [Republika](https://ekonomi.republika.co.id/berita/tn6s16370/bursa-efek-indonesia-perbarui-ketentuan-auto-reject-bawah-untuk-saham-harga-rendah)). The arithmetic explains the patch: 7% of Rp14 is Rp0.98, less than one tick, so under a pure 7% rule those stocks could not fall at all. Some outlets print the decree as "Kep-000157" with six digits; IDX's usual five-digit format makes Kep-00157 the likely number. All three decrees cover the Main, Development and New Economy boards only.

| Regime | Dates | Rp1–10 | Rp11–14 | Rp15–200 | >Rp200–5,000 | >Rp5,000 | Decree |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R2025-ARB15 | 2025-04-08 to 2026-09-25 | n/a (Rp50 floor) | 35% / 15% | 35% / 15% | 25% / 15% | 20% / 15% | Kep-00003/BEI/04-2025 |

Cells show ARA / ARB. A figure without % is a fixed rupiah limit. Before 2026-09-28, the Rp11–14 and Rp15–200 columns were a single Rp50–200 tier.

The schema has to change before these regimes can be loaded. Today the CSV holds only `ara_pct` and `arb_pct`. It needs fixed-rupiah limits (for example `ara_abs` and `arb_abs`, or a `limit_type` column), a `board` column, and a lowest tier starting at Rp10 from 28 September 2026. A symmetric ARB from 1 January 2027 had been announced in September ([Stockbit Snips](https://snips.stockbit.com/snips-terbaru/-bei-ubah-klasifikasi-auto-rejection-per-28-september-2026-arb-simetris-per-2027); [Investor.id](https://investor.id/market/454910/siapsiap-bei-terapkan-arb-simetris)). The October cut came after that announcement, so 2027 should stay unknown in the table until IDX publishes a decision. The removal of the Rp50 floor also matters for interpreting the data. Until 25 September, rows stuck at Rp50 were censored at the floor, not stable. On the first Rp1 day, 26 former Rp50 stocks fell below Rp50 in session I ([Bisnis](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20260928/7/2007761/era-saham-rp1-dimulai-26-saham-eks-gocap-berguguran-sesi-i)). A cluster of new lows that week reflects the rule change, not manipulation.

**Other boards follow their own rules and need their own rows.** The Acceleration Board, launched in 2019, has a Rp1 minimum price, a Rp1 limit for Rp1 to Rp10 and 10% above that ([Pasardana](https://pasardana.id/news/2019/5/28/papan-akselerasi-mungkinkan-investor-beli-saham-rp-1/)). A December 2020 decree reportedly kept the 10% figure ([CNBC Indonesia](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/market/20210416141902-17-238464/2-emiten-baru-sahamnya-drop-di-bawah-arb-normal-kok-bisa)). The special monitoring board moved to full call auction on 25 March 2024, with five sessions a day from Monday to Thursday and four on Friday. Only the indicative equilibrium price and volume are shown, and limits are Rp1 for Rp1 to Rp10 and 10% above that ([Bisnis](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20240325/7/1752586/bei-luncurkan-papan-pemantauan-khusus-tahap-ii-cek-detailnya)). An example is BREN, which fell its 10% limit on its first FCA day ([Bisnis](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20240529/7/1769261/saham-bren-prajogo-pangestu-langsung-arb-10-akibat-ppk-full-call-auction)). IDX proposed tiered 35/25/20% FCA limits in July 2026 ([Bisnis](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20260706/7/1985764/bei-siapkan-aturan-baru-auto-rejection-untuk-saham-fca)), but no source confirms the proposal was adopted. FCA prices come from periodic auctions, so their volume and range are not comparable with continuous trading. The 92 stocks that left the FCA board on 28 September 2026 each have a structural break on that date ([Katadata](https://katadata.co.id/finansial/bursa/6ab88bb98f54a/bei-keluarkan-92-saham-dari-fca-picu-volatilitas-saat-harga-bawah-rp-1-berlaku)).

**Several more rule changes affect how daily rows should be read.** The tick schedule moved to five price bands on 2 May 2016 (Kep-00023/BEI/04-2016): Rp1 below Rp200, Rp2 up to Rp500, Rp5 up to Rp2,000, Rp10 up to Rp5,000 and Rp25 above ([Bisnis](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20160502/190/543637/bei-berlakukan-fraksi-harga-baru-mulai-hari-ini-simak-rincian-perubahannya)). The schedule was unchanged in a February 2023 explainer ([OCBC](https://www.ocbc.id/id/article/2023/02/16/fraksi-harga-saham-adalah)), and no 2023 or 2024 tick change was found, contrary to the project brief. First-day IPO limits were twice the normal percentage until Kep-00025/BEI/03-2020 cut them to 1× from 13 March 2020 ([Pasardana](https://pasardana.id/news/2020/3/12/bei-sesuaikan-ketentuan-auto-rejection-dan-saham-yang-diperdagangkan-pada-sesi-pra-pembukaan)). How the closing price is formed has changed in stages:

- Pre-opening was suspended from 13 March 2020 and restored for LQ45 stocks only from 7 September 2020 ([Bisnis](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20200906/7/1287822/ingat-sesi-pre-opening-indeks-lq45-mulai-berlaku-besok-7-september-2020)).
- From 6 December 2021, IDX published indicative auction prices, introduced a random close, and **hid broker codes in real-time post-trade data**, which removes a public wash-trade signal ([Bisnis](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20211206/7/1474334/mekanisme-pre-closing-dan-penutupan-kode-broker-resmi-berlaku-hari-ini)).
- In December 2024, the list of stocks eligible for the pre-opening and pre-closing auctions was extended beyond LQ45 ([Bisnis](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20241203/7/1821024/ini-daftar-saham-yang-kini-bisa-ditransaksikan-di-sesi-pra-opening)).
- From December 2025, pre-closing orders cannot be amended or cancelled from 15:56 ([Bareksa](https://www.bareksa.com/berita/saham/2025-12-11/jangan-salah-input-bei-terapkan-aturan-baru-order-saham-kini-dikunci-di-jam-tertentu/amp)).

Each of these changes shifts how easy it is to mark the close, so close-versus-VWAP features need a regime indicator. Market-wide halts shorten the session. Under the April 2025 rule (Kep-00002/BEI/04-2025), the first halt comes at an 8% fall, the second at 15%, and a suspension at 20% ([Bareksa](https://www.bareksa.com/berita/saham/2025-04-08/aturan-baru-bei-soal-trading-halt-berlaku-mulai-8-april-2025-ihsg-bisa-stop-seharian)). Halt days should be excluded from baselines: six in March and September 2020, plus 18 March and 8 April 2025, 28 and 29 January 2026, and 16 October 2026 ([CNBC Indonesia](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/research/20261016095125-128-776739/perdagangan-bursa-sempat-alami-trading-halt-ini-aturan-sejarahnya); [Liputan6](https://www.liputan6.com/saham/read/8314523/ihsg-trading-halt-lagi-ini-rekam-jejak-penghentian-perdagangan-saham)). CNBC counts seven halts in 2020, while the Liputan6 list gives six dates, so one 2020 date is unresolved. Short selling began only on 15 September 2026, and reportedly no stocks are eligible for November 2026 ([Kontan](https://investasi.kontan.co.id/news/mulai-hari-ini-selasa-1592026-bei-berlakukan-short-selling-secara-bertahap); [Kontan](https://investasi.kontan.co.id/news/tak-ada-saham-yang-bisa-ditransaksikan-short-selling-pada-periode-november-2026)). For the whole sample, then, price falls cannot be explained by covered shorting.

## Where the new evidence revises the original label dataset

Most of the earlier label report survives. The PANR window and its Rp5.07bn disgorgement are reconfirmed ([StockWatch](https://stockwatch.id/ojk-sanksi-tiga-pihak-dalam-kasus-manipulasi-saham-panr-rp507-miliar-wajib-dikembalikan-dan-18-rekening-ditutup/)). So is DSSA's last window, 18 July 2024 to 3 February 2026, and OJK is now preparing its first restitution scheme for harmed DSSA investors ([Periskop](https://periskop.id/pasar-modal/20261119/ojk-siapkan-pengembalian-dana-korban-manipulasi-dssa)). The March 2020 dating also holds: Kep-00023 set 10% from 10 March and Kep-00025 set 7% from 13 March ([CNN Indonesia](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/ekonomi/20200313074512-92-483053/bursa-saham-tertekan-batas-auto-rejection-jadi-7-persen)). The detection-methods notes contain a search summary that dates a 7% ARB to 9 March 2020 under Kep-00023. The trading-rules notes and their decree-level sources contradict it, and the repo's dates should stand. Six items do change, set out in the table below.

Two further points refine the earlier report. Its reconciliation of OJK's "29 cases / Rp251.7bn / 155 parties" was arithmetic: Rp240.65bn plus Rp11.05bn, and 151 plus 4 parties. The new research could not find that figure stated anywhere, and found "32 cases" attached to the same 2022 to January 2026 period ([Investortrust](https://investortrust.id/market/94002/ojk-catat-32-kasus-manipulasi-saham-sepanjang-2022-januari-2026)). The case count should be quoted as "about 29 to 32". The earlier recommendation that the 2020 to 2023 7% ARB "truncates crash signatures" now applies again from 19 October 2026, and the README's regime table needs the same update as the CSV. Every 2026 decree number and figure above comes from multiple news extracts, not the decree PDFs. Before the CSV rows are committed, the PDFs should be read on idx.co.id, including whether a fixed Rp1 limit means "Rp1 or more" or "more than Rp1" from the reference price.

## Conclusion

The project's binding constraints have changed. Model choice is no longer the main risk: an isolation forest and a z-score screen are adequate for 13 windows, and the literature offers nothing much better at this label count. The risks are now provenance and interpretation. The best free data sits behind terms that prohibit scraping and a firewall that blocks the cloud environment the agent loop runs in. The most reproducible weak label, the ARA streak, is only as correct as a regime table that went stale twice in October 2026 alone. IDX is also changing its own surveillance mid-sample: UMA notices tripled between 2023 and 2025, suspensions are giving way to investigation announcements, and trading-based PPK criteria were removed in September 2026. So any label series built from exchange actions will show breaks driven by policy rather than behaviour.

The practical order follows from this. First, prove that `GetStockSummary` reaches 2015 from a machine IDX will serve. Then fix the regime schema with board and fixed-rupiah limits, and read the 2026 decree PDFs before committing rows. After that, build the price-only screen and score it against placebo windows, because without a null even a random list "catches" PANR and DSSA. The detector earns its keep only if it flags stocks before IDX's own UMA. NSSS, FUJI and CASA will be the first out-of-sample test of that, with results due around late December 2026.
