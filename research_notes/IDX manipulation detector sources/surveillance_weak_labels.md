## 1. IDX "Unusual Market Activity" (UMA) announcements

### Takeaway
UMA is the IDX's most common public surveillance flag. Yearly counts have grown from about 50 in 2015 to about 528 notices in 2025. The IDX publishes no numeric triggers; it says it looks at price, volume, orders, trading patterns and material information. The live IDX announcement API keeps only the last three years, so UMA history from 2015 to 2023 has to come from other sources: news, the paid TICMI archive, academic datasets, or web archives. As a label, UMA is high-recall and low-precision. The IDX itself says a UMA is not evidence of a violation.

### Cited Findings
- **Definition.** UMA is a notice the IDX issues when a stock's price or volume moves abnormally. A UMA "does not always violate regulations" and does not guarantee a suspension. [V-snippet] — [Mandiri Sekuritas](https://www.mandirisekuritas.co.id/en/siaran-pers-artikel/artikel/pengertian-uma-saham); [Digivestasi](https://www.digivestasi.com/news/detail/saham_terkini/uma-stock-suspension-rules-on-idx-cdia-and-coin-under-spotlight?lang=eng); [Kontan explainer](https://investasi.kontan.co.id/news/apa-itu-uma-pada-saham-ini-sejarah-kriteria-emiten-dan-tips-menghadapinya)
- **What the IDX considers.** An IDX official quoted by Kontan said the exchange weighs "fluctuations in price, orders, volume, transaction patterns and relevant material information, among others." No numeric thresholds are given. [V-snippet] — [Kontan, CDIA/COIN UMA and suspension](https://investasi.kontan.co.id/news/saham-cdia-dan-coin-masuk-uma-dan-kena-suspensi-begini-penjelasan-bei)
- **Typical UMA notice.** It names the stock, says recent price/volume movement is outside the usual pattern, says the IDX is monitoring, and tells investors to watch the issuer's replies to IDX queries and disclosures. The issuer is often asked to reply (UMA "replies"). Example notices: ABBA ([Tempo](https://en.tempo.co/amp/1441676/idx-considers-abba-share-surge-unusual-market-activity)), INDX ([brights.id mirror of the announcement](https://www.brights.id/en/announcement/pengumuman-unusual-market-activity-uma-atas-saham-indx)), NICE, March 2024 ([Indonesia Business Post](https://indonesiabusinesspost.com/2360/business-and-investment/idx-issues-unusual-market-activity-for-pt-adhi-kartiko-pratama-shares)). ### Inferences
- UMA counts are roughly 50 to 530 notices a year, so 2015-2026 should give somewhere around 2,000 to 2,500 stock-day flags (Inferred from the counts above; 2017-2020 counts not found). That is far more than the 13 OJK windows and makes UMA a usable high-recall weak-label channel.
- Label quality: UMA is an ex-post flag on price/volume extremes. A detector built on returns and volume will "agree" with it almost by construction. Use UMA mainly to (a) test lead time (does the detector fire before the UMA date?) and (b) stratify. Do not treat it as ground truth. Hanafi's no-reversal finding supports the view that many UMAs are informed moves, not manipulation.
- The 2024-2025 count jump coincides with the FCA watchlist and a tighter suspension policy, so the label's base rate shifts with policy. Normalise per year or per regime.
- Best path to a 2015-2023 UMA list without TICMI: (1) the Owner 2021-2023 paper and the UGM/IPB theses, which often add appendices listing UMA stocks; (2) Wayback Machine captures of the IDX UMA page and of `GetAnnouncement` JSON; (3) news aggregators that republish daily UMA lists (IPOT News, EmitenNews, paretosaham.com, Kontan). [Inferred]

### Gaps
- UMA counts for 2017-2020 not found.
- Official IDX numeric UMA criteria, and the decree that formalises UMA (the procedure sits in IDX surveillance practice, not in a public numeric rule), not found.
- No verified UMA-specific idx.co.id endpoint. History before the three-year window is reachable only via TICMI (paid) or archives.
- No public, ready-made UMA dataset covering 2015-2026 found. The Owner paper's per-stock list could not be opened (domain blocked).

## 2. IDX trading suspensions: price/volume reasons versus other reasons

### Takeaway
Suspension notices state the reason in standard Indonesian phrases, so they can be classified with text rules. Price-driven ("cooling down") suspensions use "peningkatan/penurunan harga kumulatif yang signifikan" and "dalam rangka cooling down sebagai bentuk perlindungan bagi investor". Non-price suspensions cite late financial statements, unpaid listing fees, free-float or Rule I-A breaches, going concern, corporate actions, and so on. A verified JSON endpoint lists suspensions by date range. Important for labels after Nov 2026: the IDX said in late Oct 2026 it will rely less on suspensions and more on public "investigation announcements" and account freezes.

### Cited Findings
- **Suspension endpoint:** `https://www.idx.co.id/primary/NewsAnnouncement/GetSuspension?indexFrom=1&dateFrom={start}&dateTo={end}&pageSize=9999&lang=en&type=spt`. A list of suspensions longer than 6 months is at `https://www.idx.co.id/id/perusahaan-tercatat/suspensi-6-bulan/`. [V-code] — [supertypeai/sectors_idx_suspension, scraper_engine/api_requester.py](https://github.com/supertypeai/sectors_idx_suspension)
- **Reason text rules in that scraper** (searched in the PDF text):
  - "peningkatan harga kumulatif yang signifikan" (significant cumulative price increase)
  - "penurunan harga kumulatif yang signifikan" (significant cumulative price decrease)
  - "cooling down sebagai bentuk perlindungan bagi investor"
  - If both of the first and third appear, the reason is a price-increase cooling-down suspension.
  - Non-price reasons in its sample output include "Belum memenuhi ketentuan V.1.1. dan/atau V.1.2. peraturan bursa nomor I-A" (BCIC, FASW, FISH, GGRP, Aug 2025 PDF) and "Keterlambatan..." (late filing).
  - Output schema: `symbol, suspension_date, reason, pdf_url`.

### Inferences
- Classification rule for the label table [Inferred]:
  - `price_up_suspension`: PDF text contains "peningkatan harga kumulatif".
  - `price_down_suspension`: "penurunan harga kumulatif".
  - `cooling_down`: "cooling down".
  - Otherwise `non_price`, with sub-reasons: "Laporan Keuangan"/"Keterlambatan" (late filings), "Biaya Pencatatan" (listing fee), "I-A"/"Free Float", "going concern"/"kelangsungan usaha", corporate action.
  - Use only price-driven events as weak positives. Non-price suspensions are useful as negatives or as contamination controls, since late filers are often illiquid penny stocks.
- Price suspensions are stronger evidence than UMA, because the IDX escalates to them only after a UMA fails to cool the stock (see the "already UMA but still rising" pattern, [StockWatch](https://stockwatch.id/sudah-kena-uma-tapi-harga-masih-ngebut-bei-akhirnya-suspensi-saham-ini/)). They are still ex-post price-extreme flags.
- From Nov 2026, suspension counts are expected to fall by policy, and "investigation announcements" become the new strongest exchange-level signal. NSSS, FUJI and CASA are the first three. Their review windows can serve as "alleged" windows.

### Gaps
- No official annual count of price-reason suspensions found for 2015-2025.
- `GetSuspension` probably has the same three-year limit as `GetAnnouncement` (Inferred, untested). Suspensions before about Nov 2023 then need TICMI, archives or news.
- The tickers for "six stocks, 12 Dec 2025" and "seven stocks, 30 Sep 2025" were not captured.

## 3. Special notations ("notasi khusus") and the special monitoring board (Papan Pemantauan Khusus, PPK / FCA watchlist)

### Takeaway
Notations are mostly fundamental flags (negative equity, late financial statements, disclaimer opinion, free float, and so on), plus "X" for stocks on the special monitoring board. Before 28 Sep 2026, the PPK's 11 criteria included trading-based ones: low price, low liquidity, and repeated UMA or suspension history. So PPK entries made under those criteria are partly a surveillance signal. The 28 Sep 2026 amendment removed criteria 1, 6, 7 and 10, and 92 securities left the board. Entry/exit history must be rebuilt from daily IDX announcements or news; I found no consolidated archive.

  - Notations were added to ticker displays in 2018-2019. Six more were added in Jan 2021, for 13 in total, and later more, for 17. ## 4. Cooling down, other surveillance mechanisms, and repeated ARA hits

### Takeaway
In IDX usage, "cooling down" means a short suspension, usually one session or one day the first time, escalating with repeats. It is triggered by significant cumulative price rises, typically after runs of consecutive ARA (upper auto-rejection) days. A run of consecutive ARA days can be computed directly from daily prices and the auto-rejection regime table already in `data/price_limit_regimes.csv`. That gives a reproducible weak label available back to 2015, independent of announcement archives.

### Cited Findings
- **Purpose.** The IDX suspends stocks with unusual cumulative rises "as a cooling down" so investors can digest information. A head of the IDX trading surveillance division (initials YAS) is quoted calling it part of the cooling-down mechanism. [V-snippet] — [Bisnis, CDIA/COIN](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20250717/7/1893965/ara-terus-menerus-bei-suspensi-saham-chandra-daya-cdia-dan-indokripto-coin)
- **Pattern.** First suspension is short (one day; NATO, RATU, DAAZ). The second is about a week and may reopen on FCA. The third can be about a month. [V-snippet] — [Kontan Insight](https://insight.kontan.co.id/news/sama-seperti-daaz-ratu-disuspensi-sehari-oleh-bei-pasca-cetak-ara-berturut-turut); [EmitenNews series](https://emitennews.com/news/ara-berkali-kali-dua-saham-kena-suspensi); [IDX Channel](https://www.idxchannel.com/market-news/lima-saham-kena-suspensi-usai-cetak-ara-beruntun/all)
- **ARA-run triggers in news:** CDIA and COIN were suspended after 6 straight ARA days (Jul 2025). A KOCI-related piece describes "2x ARA and 8x consecutive ARA" before suspension. [V-snippet] — [Investortrust](https://investortrust.id/market/72806/ara-6-hari-beruntun-bei-suspensi-saham-cdia-dan-coin); [EmitenNews](https://emitennews.com/news/sempat-2x-ara-8x-ara-beruntun-2-saham-disuspensi-bei-kapan-dibuka)
- **Auto-rejection regimes** (needed to compute ARA hits): 2015 asymmetric regime under Kep-00096/BEI/08-2015 (ARA 35/25/20%, ARB 10%); symmetric from 3 Jan 2017 under Kep-00113/BEI/12-2016; later changes. [V-code, project file] — `/home/user/saham-gorengan/data/price_limit_regimes.csv`

### Inferences
- Proposed weak label `ara_run_k`: a stock-day closes at the ARA price for k or more consecutive sessions (k = 3 to 6), using the regime table. The 2025 suspensions above suggest the IDX usually acts at about 4 to 8 consecutive ARA days. This label is fully reproducible over 2015-2026 from OHLC data alone, but it is a price-extreme label (same circularity caveat as UMA). Recent IPOs (DAAZ, RATU, CDIA, COIN, KOCI) dominate ARA runs, so add a "days since listing" control. [Inferred]
- The chain "UMA → cooling-down suspension → repeat suspension → FCA" is a natural severity ladder for weak labels: UMA = 1, first price suspension = 2, repeat suspension or FCA after a suspension = 3, IDX investigation announcement = 4, OJK sanction or court verdict = 5. [Inferred]
- IDX pre-opening and closing call auctions and the random closing (introduced to curb "marking the close") are other mechanisms, but they produce no per-stock public flag. [Inferred; not researched in depth]

### Gaps
- No written IDX rule found that fixes cooling-down durations or ARA-count triggers. Practice is described only by media and IDX officials.
- No official counts of cooling-down suspensions per year.
